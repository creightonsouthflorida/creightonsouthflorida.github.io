    Frequently Asked Questions

    We know it can be scary to try new things. Here are a few questions we are often asked.

    How does this system even work? Everyone says the rhythm method is ineffective.

    The Creighton Model System is not the rhythm method. It uses a prospective accumulation of objective, standardized data on the menstrual and fertility cycles, based on the physical signs a woman has when she is fertile. Couples use that information to plan or avoid pregnancy by knowing whether each day is fertile or infertile. The system has a 99.6% effectiveness to avoid pregnancy and a very high accuracy for achieving pregnancy.

    I have irregular cycles. Will Creighton work for me?

    Yes. Each person is different, but irregular cycles are often a symptom of underlying medical issues. Birth control pills mask the symptoms, while Creighton gets to the heart of the issues in a natural and healthy way.

    Won't this ruin my sex life? What about spontaneity?

    Couples using CrMS report an increase in communication and growth in the SPICE dimensions of their relationship. Avoiding pregnancy does require periodic abstinence, but periods of abstinence are normal in every marriage — illness, travel, pregnancy. During those times CrMS allows couples to become more generous, attentive, respectful and loving. As confidence and proficiency grow, ovulation becomes easier to predict and there are more opportunities for spontaneity.

    Can I download charting material from this site?

    No. Creighton is a highly standardized system, so we want to make sure you are comfortable charting and doing it correctly. If you need new charts or stamps, contact your practitioner.

    Is there an app for charting?

    Yes — once you have completed five follow-up sessions with your practitioner you get access to the official Creighton app. Paper charts are the easiest way to start charting consistently, so we start there first.
